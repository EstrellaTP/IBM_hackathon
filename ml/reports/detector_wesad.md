# Episode detector on real WESAD wrist data

Generated by `python3 -m ml.models.detector_evaluation` on LinuxONE. Stress event = contiguous TSST label; true detection = episode detected during stress or up to 60 s after it; every other episode is a false alarm (baseline, amusement and meditation count as non-stress). One stress event per subject.

- **LOSO** is the honest estimate: the thresholds for each subject were chosen on the other 14 subjects only (best pooled event F1, ties broken by fewer false alarms per hour).
- **Deployed defaults** is the configuration chosen in most folds, evaluated on all 15 subjects (in-sample, so optimistic).
- Deployed defaults: `baseline_alpha=0.02`, `hr_z_threshold=2.0`, `activity_z_max=0.5`, `movement_lookback_seconds=180`, `eda_z_min=1.0`, `hr_z_threshold_without_eda=2.5`.
- **HR-only band** rows replay the same data with EDA removed, as a heart-rate-only wearable would send it; the EDA check is then skipped and `hr_z_threshold_without_eda` applies.

## Limitations

- WESAD is a laboratory protocol with 15 young, healthy participants mostly sitting; it shows whether the personal baseline catches acute stress, not behaviour in daily life (the nurse dataset covers that, F3-ML-03).
- One stress event per subject: event recall moves in steps of 1/15.
- Heart rate is the Empatica E4's own 1 Hz estimate from BVP.
